Gilded Shadow — a top-down stealth game set in the Meridian Gallery after dark. You are Wren, a thief with three galleries to empty: slip past patrolling guards and their vision cones, stay out of the lamplight, lift every relic, and reach the green exit unseen. Toss a coin to pull a guard away, sneak to stay quiet, and mind the eye above — a guard who sees you fills it, and a full eye means a chase.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrows to move, hold Shift to sneak, click to toss a coin, P to pause, R to restart.
